# Pumpkin Session-Only Adult Age Gate Preview Standard V2.8.62F

Adult-preview acknowledgement is an accessibility and review control, not final legal acceptance or an identity-verification system.

## State and Privacy

- Store acknowledgement in sessionStorage only and show the gate again in every new browser context.
- Do not use localStorage, durable cookies, backend persistence, accounts, analytics containing personal data, or date-of-birth collection.
- Do not collect name, email, phone, or any other personal field.

## Accessibility and Interaction

- Make background content inert before acknowledgement, move focus into the gate, trap focus, support keyboard operation, and restore usable navigation after acknowledgement.
- Provide clear 21+ acknowledgement and leave actions.
- Prove fresh-session gating, same-session continuity, and new-session re-gating in browser automation.

## Hold

- Owner/legal review is required before public launch; preview proof does not approve production language or regulatory sufficiency.
