# Partner Feedback Carryforward

The owner/partner reported four functional gaps after the initial visual acceptance:

1. Home categories incorrectly opened the contact form.
2. Item cards incorrectly opened the contact form.
3. Catalog was absent from the top navigation.
4. Static-reference item pages were not represented in the live route graph.

OSHR treats that feedback as superseding the earlier OSG/OSH visual-only acceptance. All four gaps are repaired in the deployed runtime. The OSG colors, typography, page chrome, responsive composition, and media treatment were retained.

This phase does not claim final owner acceptance on behalf of the partner. It records a technically complete repair ready for owner review, subject to the remaining form-reproof and CMS-persistence holds.
