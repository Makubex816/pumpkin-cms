# Wizard Access Control

Suggested roles:

- business owner
- content reviewer
- legal/privacy reviewer
- operator
- developer/admin
- indexing owner

Role rules:

- business owners can fill intake and approve content.
- legal reviewers can approve legal/privacy sections.
- operators can run validators and prepare approval packages.
- only authorized admins can perform external mutation gates.
- indexing owner must approve final Search Console/indexing.
