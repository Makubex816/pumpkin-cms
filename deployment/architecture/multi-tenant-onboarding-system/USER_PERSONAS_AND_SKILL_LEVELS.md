# User Personas and Skill Levels

## Business Owner

Low computer skill, high business knowledge. Provides business name, services, locations, contact details, page approval, legal/privacy decisions, and final signoff.

Needs plain words, examples, checklists, and a clear list of what not to share.

## Content Reviewer

May not know the CMS internals. Reviews copy, photos, service claims, page routes, and placeholder/outdated content.

Needs side-by-side review links, simple approval buttons, and a way to mark changes.

## Operator

Can follow technical steps safely but may not know all code paths. Runs validators, prepares support packets, requests approvals, and performs approved external actions.

Needs runbooks, checklists, expected outputs, and rollback notes.

## Developer

Builds validators, CLI, Admin UI, import tooling, deployment profile automation, and extension pack support.

Needs schemas, stable contracts, failure modes, test expectations, and migration rules.

## Compliance or Legal Reviewer

Reviews privacy, consent, data retention, accessibility basics, media usage, claims, and analytics/tracking implications.

Needs a focused checklist and explicit risk status.
