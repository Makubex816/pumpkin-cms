# Step 06: Review and Approvals

Approvals protect the business from accidental changes.

You may be asked to approve:

- content import
- media setup
- form setup
- staging review
- production cutover
- owner review before indexing
- final Search Console/indexing

Only approve the step you understand. If the approval text says DNS, deployment, email, or indexing and you are not ready for that, do not approve it.

An approval should say exactly what is allowed and what is not allowed.
