# Search Console and Indexing Final Gate

## Rule

Search Console submission, sitemap submission, URL Inspection, indexing request, and indexing monitoring are final completion tasks only.

They must not occur until:

- production smoke tests pass
- sitemap/robots/canonical/noindex checks pass
- obsolete/preview routes are excluded
- hidden draft/review/admin public payloads are absent
- manual content approval is recorded
- legal/privacy review is recorded or risk-accepted
- form oversight is assigned
- analytics/tracking decision is recorded
- monitoring and rollback ownership are assigned
- the user gives explicit final indexing approval

## Future Final Approval

Final approval should explicitly name the allowed Search Console actions and continue to exclude unrelated systems.

## Non-Authorization

Technical readiness does not authorize indexing. A validator may say the site is technically ready; the final gate still remains closed until human approval exists.
