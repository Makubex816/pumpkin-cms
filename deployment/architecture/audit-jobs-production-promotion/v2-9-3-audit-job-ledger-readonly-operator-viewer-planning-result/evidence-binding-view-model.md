# Evidence Binding View Model

Evidence binding rows are derived from `ledger.evidenceBindings`.

## Fields

- `id`
- `type`
- `sourceRef`
- `safePath`
- `summary`
- `hasArtifactHash`

## Purpose

The evidence panel shows the safe evidence map without exposing private data, raw form payloads, tokens, credentials, connection strings, or protected config.

## Combined Fixture

The combined fixture exposes 13 evidence bindings.
