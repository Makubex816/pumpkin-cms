# Atlas Operating Contract v3

The Atlas is the durable map of system truth, not a replacement for Git, deployments, databases, or live readback. Every claim links to evidence and carries a status/freshness classification.

## Major milestone invariant

```text
Milestone complete
IFF
implementation/result closeout is complete
AND evidence index is updated
AND capability/milestone/lineage maps are updated
AND Atlas validates
AND current state and resumption capsule agree
AND CHAT-PACK regenerates
AND ZIP and SHA-256 regenerate
```

The package Atlas remains a proposed bridge until the active-build Atlas is ingested. Preserve existing IDs, history, and update tooling. Migrate through an explicit adapter; never overwrite unseen history.

New v3 Atlas subjects include upstream qualification state, CAPTCHA foundation/gaps/rollout, visual editor/block identity/navigation/header media, current-build closeout, public lineage versus active source, chat migration readiness, and the Authorize.Net external gate.
