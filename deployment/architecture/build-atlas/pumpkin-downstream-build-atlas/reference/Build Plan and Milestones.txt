The internal FormEntry must be the authoritative lead record. Email notification must be secondary: a missing, delayed, or failed email sender must never prevent the lead from being saved or make it disappear.

The universal closeout will require, for every current and future tenant:

- Every live form instance maps to a valid active canonical FormDefinition.
- A successful submission creates exactly one tenant-scoped FormEntry.
- TenantAdmins can list, open, update status, export, and manage only their own tenant’s leads.
- SuperAdmins can access and manage leads across all tenants.
- The same FormEntry ID is inaccessible through another tenant.
- Correlation IDs, submission IDs, and idempotency prevent duplicates and ambiguous timeouts.
- Form persistence works even when no external email provider exists.
- Contact and notification emails are mutable tenant settings, separate from login identity.
