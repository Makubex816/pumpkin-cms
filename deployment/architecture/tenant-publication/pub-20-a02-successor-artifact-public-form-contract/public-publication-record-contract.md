# Public publication record contract

PublicPublication records publication ID, tenant UID, release, active/revoked state, indexing state, exact origins and hostnames, form mappings, validity bounds, revision, and audit metadata. Browser tenant IDs are never authority.

Live publication pub20-a02-pilot binds tenant pub20-a02-synthetic, release pub20-a02-release-v2, mapping pub20-a02-contact, and origin https://purple-mud-029914d0f.7.azurestaticapps.net.
