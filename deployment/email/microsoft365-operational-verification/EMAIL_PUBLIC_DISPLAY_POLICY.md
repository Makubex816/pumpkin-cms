# Email Public Display Policy

This file records the public email display decision for IceSkatingRinkRentals.com.

## Current Known State

- `contact@iceskatingrinkrentals.com` exists or is intended as the primary Microsoft 365 mailbox/user.
- Manual Outlook access, outbound send, and inbound reply tests are confirmed working for `contact@iceskatingrinkrentals.com`.
- Public email display is still under review.
- Form-first contact remains recommended.
- Phone display decision remains separate.
- Contact/service pages remain on hold.
- No CMS Page records are changed by this readiness package.

## Options

### A. Form-First Only

Do not display public email. Use the contact/quote form as the main public contact path.

Pros:

- Reduces spam exposure.
- Keeps Pumpkin Lead Inbox as the primary lead source.
- Avoids premature mailbox monitoring expectations.

Cons:

- Some visitors prefer direct email.

### B. Display Contact Email On Contact Page Only

Display `contact@iceskatingrinkrentals.com` only on the contact page after mailbox monitoring is verified.

Pros:

- Gives direct email access in an expected place.
- Limits sitewide scraping exposure.

Cons:

- Still increases spam and monitoring requirements.

### C. Display Contact Email Sitewide

Display `contact@iceskatingrinkrentals.com` in the footer or sitewide chrome.

Pros:

- Very visible direct contact path.

Cons:

- Highest spam exposure.
- Should wait for monitoring, filtering, and staffing policy approval.

### D. Obfuscated Email Or Mailto Later

Use an obfuscated display or `mailto:` link later after policy approval.

Pros:

- Can balance usability and spam resistance.

Cons:

- Still requires monitoring and implementation review.

## Recommendation

Use form-first for launch.

Do not display public email sitewide until spam handling, monitoring, reply ownership, and public contact policy are approved.

Use `contact@iceskatingrinkrentals.com` internally for replies and mailbox identity while the public display policy remains under review.

## Current Decision

- Public email display: under review.
- CMS/page import impact: no direct CMS change.
